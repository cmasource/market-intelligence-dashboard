# Financial Formula Placeholders

This document tracks formulas planned for the CMA Market Intelligence calculation engine. The MVP uses mock values only.

## Fundamental ratios

- P/E: market price per share divided by earnings per share.
- P/B: market price per share divided by book value per share.
- ROE: net income divided by shareholders equity.
- ROA: net income divided by total assets.
- EPS/BPA: net income attributable to common shareholders divided by weighted average shares.
- EBITDA margin: EBITDA divided by revenue.
- Dividend yield: annual dividend per share divided by market price per share.

## CAPM and Beta

- Expected return: risk-free rate plus beta multiplied by market risk premium.
- Beta: covariance of asset returns with market returns divided by market variance.
- Alpha placeholder for realized return versus CAPM expected return.
