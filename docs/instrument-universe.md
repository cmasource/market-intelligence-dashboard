# Instrument universe

CMA Market Intelligence keeps a normalized instrument universe for search, screener, heatmap and local-market navigation.

## Segments

- USA equities and ETFs with provider/fallback quote support where available.
- CEDEAR references that connect local Argentine context with the USA underlying.
- Argentine equities from common BYMA panels.
- Sovereign bond species such as AL30, AL30D, AL30C, GD30, GD30D and GD30C.
- CER and LECAP examples with indexation separated from currency.
- Crypto assets supported by the existing provider/fallback chain.

## Argentina data status

Argentina local prices can be manual, structured mock or future provider coverage. The platform is designed to replace simulated values progressively with validated manual loads, BYMA/IOL/PPI/licensed provider integrations, and CNV document context where applicable.
