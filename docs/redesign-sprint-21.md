# Sprint 21 Redesign

Sprint 21 moves CMA Market Intelligence away from a generic dashboard look and toward a distinctive premium fintech/trading intelligence platform.

## Direction implemented

The new direction is **CMA Institutional Fintech Terminal**:

- deep navy/black institutional base;
- cyan/teal primary accent and electric blue secondary accent;
- violet AI/analytics accent;
- green positive, amber caution and rose negative signals;
- subtle market grid overlays and radial glows;
- wider dashboard pages and cleaner shareable report pages;
- differentiated card families for price, analysis, risk, news, Argentina/CEDEAR and fixed income.

## Reference use

Basedash, AxCorp and Cryptolly were used only as mood and structural references:

- Basedash for BI clarity, sticky header, centered command surfaces and hierarchy.
- AxCorp/Cryptolly for trading/crypto energy and premium market tone.

No third-party assets, template layouts, proprietary CSS, logos, videos, copy or exact gradients were copied.

## Updated surfaces

- Global theme tokens and utility classes in `app/globals.css`.
- Wider `AppShell` container system.
- Sticky glass header with active route indicator.
- Homepage hero command center and terminal-style search.
- Differentiated market pulse and featured asset cards.
- Asset page rhythm with stronger quote, coverage, intelligence, chart, technical/fundamental, CEDEAR/news/risk hierarchy.
- Report pages retain a narrower executive brief feel.
- Argentina, screener, data audit and status pages use intentional panel/card treatments.

## Non-advisory stance

The redesign changes presentation only. CMA Market Intelligence remains informational and does not provide investment advice. Existing signal wording such as Neutral, Constructive and Very constructive remains non-recommendation language.
